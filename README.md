Garchi CMS is a content management system for everything a business publishes: website and app content, blog posts and social posts. A team edits in one place, AI assistants draft, and only a person can publish a page.

**Areas:** CMS · Content Management · Headless CMS · Marketing · Digital Publishing

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Garchi CMS: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
